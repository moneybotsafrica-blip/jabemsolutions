How to use this dashboard

  1. Choose a start and end date, then select Apply date range.
  2. Open a report for detailed records or download its Excel workbook.
  3. Set product cost prices under Products for accurate gross-profit figures.
  4. Review low and out-of-stock items before placing new orders.

Metric definitions

Gross profit is revenue less product cost. Inventory cost value is on-hand quantity multiplied by unit cost. Cancelled orders are excluded from sales and P&L totals.
